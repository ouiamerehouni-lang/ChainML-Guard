✅ A) OFFLINE THRESHOLDS COMPUTATION SCRIPT
   Location: /scripts/compute_thresholds.py

   - Uses TRAIN split only (80/20, random_state=42, stratify=y)
   - Computes data-driven percentiles:
     • age_p10: 10th percentile of wallet age
     • tx_p90: 90th percentile of transaction count
     • bal_p05: 5th percentile of balance
     • bal_p95: 95th percentile of balance
     • rate_p90: 90th percentile of activity rate (tx/age)
   - Saves to thresholds.json in project root

✅ B) REASON SUMMARY FUNCTION
   Location: /utils/explanations.py
   Function: generate_reason_summary(balance, tx_count, wallet_age_days, thresholds)
   
   Returns list of human-readable reasons based on heuristic rules:
   - "Very new wallet (age below 10th percentile)"
   - "High activity for its age (activity rate above 90th percentile)"
   - "Unusually high transaction count (above 90th percentile)"
   - "Unusually low balance (below 5th percentile)"
   - "Unusually high balance (above 95th percentile)"
   - "No strong risk indicators from the available features" (fallback)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                            TECHNICAL NOTES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Why Percentiles?
  - Robust to outliers
  - Adapt to dataset distribution
  - More reliable than fixed thresholds

Why Training Set Only?
  - Avoids data leakage
  - Represents model's training data
  - Prevents overfitting to test set

Why Heuristics Instead of SHAP/LIME?
  - Faster (instant vs. seconds per prediction)
  - More transparent (simple rules vs. complex math)
  - Easier to explain to non-technical users
  - No need for additional model calls

Why Activity Rate (tx/age)?
  - More informative than tx_count alone
  - Captures "burst" behavior of bots
  - Classic indicator of automated wallets
